Marshmallow Cold Foam: A Small-Batch Coffee Topping

Make marshmallow cold foam for iced coffee with a small dairy batch, frothing tips, three flavor variations and easy texture adjustments.

Marshmallow cold foam adds a soft, sweet cap to iced coffee without turning the whole drink into a thick shake. A small batch is easiest to adjust: combine a little marshmallow creme with cold dairy, froth briefly and spoon it over coffee just before serving. This is an original home recipe, not a tested café formula.

Ingredients for one drink

Use 1 tablespoon marshmallow creme, 2 tablespoons cold heavy cream and 1 tablespoon cold milk. For the coffee, prepare 150–180 ml cooled strong coffee or cold brew, ice and optional milk to taste. Start without additional syrup because the creme already contributes sweetness.

A small jar or narrow cup helps a handheld frother reach the mixture. Marshmallow creme and whole marshmallows behave differently, so use the spreadable creme for this version. Check the product ingredients if you have dietary restrictions.

Mix before frothing

Stir the marshmallow creme with the milk until it is as smooth as you can make it, then add the cream. Keeping the ingredients cold helps the foam hold a softer body. If the creme stays in lumps, mix patiently with a spoon before using the frother.

Froth for about 15–30 seconds, stopping as soon as the mixture becomes thick but pourable. Tool power varies, so look at the texture rather than the clock alone. Do not continue until it becomes a stiff whipped topping.

Build the iced coffee

Fill a glass with ice and pour in the cooled coffee. Add a little milk if desired, then spoon the foam over the top. Leave some space at the rim so you can drink or stir without spilling.

Taste the coffee before adding extra sweetness. Stronger coffee often balances the marshmallow more effectively than adding more cream. The foam gradually blends into the drink, which is part of the serving experience rather than a problem to solve.

Three simple variations

For vanilla, add one or two drops of vanilla extract to the foam. For a cocoa version, dissolve 1 teaspoon cocoa into a small amount of hot coffee, then cool it before assembling the drink. A light cinnamon dusting gives a warmer accent without changing the foam base.

Use decaf coffee for the same flavor direction with less caffeine. Plant-based whipping products may work, but different formulations will not froth identically; test a small amount and follow the product's directions instead of expecting a direct dairy replacement.

Troubleshoot and serve promptly

If the foam is thin, use colder ingredients and froth briefly again. If it is too thick, stir in a teaspoon of milk. If it is too sweet, reduce the marshmallow creme next time rather than diluting the entire drink with lots of water.

Make the topping close to serving and keep dairy cold until needed. Refrigerate unused dairy promptly and follow its packaging guidance. This recipe does not establish a storage life for mixed foam. The AI image shows a serving idea, and the actual foam depth depends on your ingredients and tool.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use whole marshmallows?

This version uses spreadable marshmallow creme. Whole marshmallows need a different method and may leave lumps.

Why did it turn into whipped cream?

It may have been frothed too long. Stop when the topping is thick and pourable; loosen it with a little milk if needed.

Can I make it with decaf?

Yes. Use decaf coffee and assemble the drink in the same way.
