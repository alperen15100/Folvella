Editorial Policy

Updated October 4, 2026

Original content and imagery

We create our article text and visual concepts originally with AI assistance. We do not republish, scrape or lightly rewrite third-party articles, and we do not use stock photographs from other websites. Original generated visuals are stored locally and described with relevant alternative text. Each illustrated example should have a distinct image that matches its explanation.

Official Pinterest research

Only official Pinterest pages are used for trend discovery. Search interest, Pin saves and outbound clicks are different measurements. We record the country, period and metric whenever available, and do not present annual predictions as today’s viral activity. Evergreen guides may remain useful without a verified growth statistic. Topic selection does not make Pinterest an endorsement of Trendora.

Practical, transparent guidance

Guides explain the decisions behind an idea rather than repeating keywords or padding a numbered list. We do not invent product testing, expert credentials, personal experience, audience numbers or performance claims. Generated images are styling references and can contain imperfect details; verify measurements and product instructions before following a concept. Recipes and styling suggestions are not represented as professionally tested unless actual testing is documented.

Dates and corrections

Publication dates identify when a guide first appeared. Update dates change when its content or visual examples receive a substantive revision. Broken links, misleading wording and image mismatches should be corrected. Report an issue through the contact page and include the article URL and the detail that needs attention.

Commercial transparency

The site currently has no active paid advertising or affiliate tracking links. Any future sponsored placement or commission-bearing link must be clearly disclosed near the relevant content. Search metadata and structured data describe actual page content; they are not promises of search rankings.
