EDITORIAL POLICY

Useful ideas, clearly presented.

Trendora publishes visual lifestyle guides designed to help readers discover, compare and save practical ideas for home, beauty, food, fashion, DIY and seasonal living.

Research and topic selection

We use current search interest, seasonal relevance, reader intent and publicly available trend signals to identify subjects worth covering. Trend signals guide topic selection; they are not presented as proof that a product, style or technique is objectively best.

Writing and review

Drafting and research may be assisted by AI tools. Articles are reviewed for clarity, internal consistency, usefulness, image-to-text relevance and obvious factual or safety issues before publication. We avoid inventing personal testing or firsthand experience that did not occur.

Images

We prioritize licensed or openly usable photography from sources such as Unsplash and Pexels and aim to match each image closely to the surrounding text. We replace imagery that appears misleading, distorted or unrelated to the idea being described.

Commerce and advertising

Trendora may earn money from display ads, affiliate links and sponsored content. Affiliate relationships are disclosed and do not change the price a reader pays. Sponsored content will be identified.

Corrections and updates

We update guides when information becomes stale, images no longer fit the text, links break or a material error is identified. Readers can report issues through the Contact page.
