## Evidence and limits

Checked official Pinterest sources and three comparable publications. A search increase, a Pin save, a profile impression and an outbound website visit are different metrics. No independently verified million-view individual Pin was found in this research. Public profile counts and third-party revenue estimates are not treated as traffic proof. Report figures below are Pinterest's reported search growth; the August report is not a daily ranking, and its retrieved text does not specify the comparison period or region.

## Sharing order and measurement

Start with handmade accents, then home café, then seasonal dressing and beauty. This order is an editorial judgment based on the observed topic signals and existing asset readiness, not a prediction of guaranteed views. Share distinct designs with relevant descriptions and the exact matching guide URL. Use original guide images where no dedicated vertical design exists; do not mislabel them as new Pin designs.

Measure impressions, saves, outbound clicks and landing-page visits separately when Pinterest account analytics and website analytics are available. This change does not connect an account, create boards, schedule posts or claim that any Pin has been published. The user requested only Pin sharing on the site; the browser's Pinterest creation page performs the final account action.

Pinterest's own Anna B. Albury case study reports a 4,700% impression increase and nearly 3,000% more engagements during an editorially featured month in 2025. This is a specific historical outcome, not an expected result for Folvella.
